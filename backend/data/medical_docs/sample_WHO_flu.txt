
WHO Influenza (Seasonal) Guidelines.

Key Facts:
- Influenza is an acute viral infection.
- Symptoms: fever, cough, headache, muscle and joint pain, severe malaise, sore throat and a runny nose.
- Transmission: easily spread from person to person via respiratory droplets.
- Prevention: Annual vaccination is the most effective way to prevent influenza.
- Treatment: Antiviral drugs such as oseltamivir can reduce complications.

High Risk Groups:
- Pregnant women
- Children under 59 months
- Elderly
- Individuals with chronic medical conditions
